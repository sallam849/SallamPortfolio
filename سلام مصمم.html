About Me

MAHMOUDASLLAM

I’m a multidisciplinary graphic designer based in Medina with over 2 years of experience in designing visual identities and digital experiences. My approach combines strategic thinking with an artistic sense. I believe great design should be invisible – it should feel inevitable. Every project starts with thorough research and ends with precise execution down to the last pixel.

I specialize in printing cardboard boxes of various sizes and shapes, and creating models in forex, foam, and acrylic.
